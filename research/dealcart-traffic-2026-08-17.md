# DealCart.io traffic/company scale signals

Date researched: 2026-08-17

## Bottom line

Public evidence does **not** support increasing portfolio claims beyond **100K+ daily users / 50K concurrent users**. I found public evidence of meaningful company/app scale, including 1M+ Google Play installs, ~14.5K Google Play reviews, a 51-200 employee LinkedIn company size, $7.5M+ disclosed funding, and press-reported revenue/order-level-profitability growth. I did **not** find first-party, app-store, investor, press, or reputable analytics evidence that confirms daily active users above 100K or concurrent users above 50K.

The strongest public claim is still app-install scale, not active-user or concurrency scale. Concurrency in particular should not be raised without internal observability, load-test, or analytics evidence.

## Source-backed findings

- **Google Play listing:** DealCart's Android listing shows **1M+ downloads**, a **4.0** rating, and roughly **14.5K-14.6K reviews**. This supports broad install/reach language, but installs are cumulative and do not prove daily users or concurrent users.  
  Source: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.localpk.android

- **Apple App Store listing:** DealCart has an iOS listing, but public iOS signal is small in the visible US App Store page: **4 ratings**, 5.0 rating, and no public install count. This does not support a larger active-user claim.  
  Source: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/dealcart/id6751234427

- **LinkedIn company page:** DealCart's LinkedIn page lists **19K+ followers**, **51-200 employees**, headquarters in Karachi, and founding year 2022. This supports startup/company scale but not user traffic or concurrency.  
  Source: https://pk.linkedin.com/company/dealcart

- **Official/investor LinkedIn post:** Shorooq's post on the 2024 seed round says DealCart raised **$3M** and calls it the fastest-growing social ecommerce platform in Pakistan. It says the investment could help impact “millions in Pakistan,” but does not report active users, daily users, monthly users, orders/day, or concurrent users.  
  Source: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/shorooqpartners_shorooqpartners-dealcart-funding-activity-7214588018131628032-UdKt

- **DealCart LinkedIn post:** DealCart's own 2022 funding announcement says the company raised **$4.5M pre-seed**, one of the larger MENAP early-stage rounds, and describes its target market. It does not disclose user, traffic, or concurrency numbers.  
  Source: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/dealcart_dealcart-is-focused-on-price-conscious-pakistani-activity-6958316626320498688-o3TK

- **TechCrunch, 2022:** TechCrunch reported DealCart raised **$4.5M pre-seed** three months after operational launch and had **$1.1M ARR** at the pre-seed stage. Useful for early traction, but it is old and not an active-user or concurrency metric.  
  Source: https://techcrunch.com/2022/07/27/dealcart-is-focused-on-price-conscious-pakistani-consumers/

- **Profit by Pakistan Today, 2024:** Profit reported DealCart raised **$3.4M / $3M seed tranche**, had grown **over 400% in dollar terms** in the prior year, was **order-level profitable**, used a single-warehouse model, and reported NPS around **55-65**. These are strong business/operating scale signals, but again no DAU/MAU/concurrency disclosure.  
  Source: https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2024/07/04/social-commerce-platform-dealcart-closes-a-3-million-round

- **Associated Press of Pakistan / company news release, 2024:** APP reported the **$3M seed** and DealCart's mission to expand reach to low- and middle-income consumers across Pakistan. It attributes the announcement to a company news release, but does not disclose users, orders/day, or concurrent users.  
  Source: https://www.app.com.pk/business/dealcart-raises-3-0-mln-to-empower-pakistans-digitally-savvy-consumers/

- **Investor/current partnership signal:** Shorooq posted in 2025 that DealCart went live on Yango, positioning it as grocery delivery through Yango's superapp and saying the channel brings essentials closer to millions of households. This is distribution reach language, not measured DealCart usage.  
  Source: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/shorooqpartners_shorooq-venturecapital-credit-privateequity-investments-dealcart-yango-grocerydelivery-pakistan-activity-7350418864007970817-qshX

- **Third-party web traffic estimate:** Hypestat estimates dealcart.io at about **14.4K monthly visits**, **476 daily visitors**, and **1.5K daily pageviews**, while also showing Similarweb/SEMrush-derived monthly estimate fields. These web estimates are low and explicitly estimated; they likely undercount app/WhatsApp commerce activity, but they do not support increasing traffic claims.  
  Source: https://hypestat.com/info/dealcart.io

- **Third-party Android aggregators:** AndroidRank/AppBrain mirror Google Play scale and show roughly **1M+ to ~2M downloads** and ~14K ratings. These are useful corroboration for cumulative install scale only.  
  Sources: https://www.androidrank.org/developer?id=DealCart and https://www.appbrain.com/dev/DealCart/

## Recommendation for portfolio wording

Keep the claim at **100K+ daily users / 50K concurrent users** only if that claim is based on internal analytics, infrastructure telemetry, load testing, or employer-approved metrics. From public sources alone, I would not increase it.

Safer public-source-backed alternatives:

- “Scaled DealCart across a 1M+ install Android app ecosystem.”
- “Supported a high-growth grocery commerce platform with 1M+ Android installs and 14K+ public reviews.”
- “Built for a venture-backed grocery commerce platform with $7.5M+ disclosed funding, 51-200 employees, and public reports of 400%+ YoY dollar growth.”

Avoid, unless backed internally:

- Any claim above **100K+ DAU**.
- Any claim above **50K concurrent users**.
- “Millions of active users” or “millions of customers.” Public sources use “millions” mainly as addressable-market or potential-impact language, not verified DealCart usage.
